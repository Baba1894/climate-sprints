When a seat has to be filled

Fractional and interim executives

A senior seat filled in weeks, without a search.

When it applies

Three situations, all of them urgent.

Sometimes a portfolio company does not need advice. It needs someone in the seat.

A departure at the wrong moment. A capability the team has never had and cannot yet justify hiring full time. A transition that needs an operator who has done it before and has no career stake in the outcome.

Configurations

Scoped to the gap, not to a job title.

  • Chief Transformation Officer — the passage from technology to product-market fit to scale
  • Commercial and go-to-market leadership — where the pipeline has to be built, not managed
  • Capital and transaction leadership — raise preparation, structuring, M&A process
  • Technical and project development leadership — first-of-a-kind deployment, project finance structuring
  • Technology and commercial assessment — a standing read across the portfolio: what the companies are actually building, where the gaps are, and which positions are drifting from the thesis they were bought on
  • ESG, carbon and data governance — reporting, controls, and a KPI architecture that survives an audit
