Privacy notice

This notice explains how [LEGAL ENTITY], trading as Climate Sprints, handles personal data collected through climatesprints.com. We are the data controller.

We do not sell personal data. We do not run advertising on this site and we do not share your details with advertisers or data brokers. We send only what you asked for.

What we collect

Information you give us

Through the sign-up form: first name, last name, company name (optional), email address, the categories you select, and anything you write in the free-text field. Through our scheduling link: your name, email, and the details you supply when booking. Through direct correspondence: whatever you choose to send us.

Information collected automatically

If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics 4 collects usage data including pages viewed, approximate location derived from IP address, device and browser type, and referring source. Analytics do not run until you consent, and you can withdraw consent at any time.

Our hosting provider processes technical logs, including IP addresses, for security and to deliver the site. This happens regardless of cookie choices and is necessary for the site to function.

When you submit the contact form, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that the submission comes from a person rather than an automated script. It examines signals from your browser and your IP address and returns a pass or a fail. It runs on every submission whether or not you accept analytics cookies, because without it the form cannot be kept open. It does not track you across other websites and is not used for advertising or profiling.

Why we use it, and on what basis

  • To reply to you and to discuss a possible engagement. Legitimate interest in responding to an enquiry you initiated, and steps taken at your request prior to a contract.
  • To send you the updates you selected. Your consent, withdrawable at any time.
  • To understand how the site is used. Your consent to analytics cookies.
  • To keep the site secure and available. Legitimate interest in operating and protecting the service.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations. Compliance with a legal obligation.

Who processes it

We use a small number of service providers, each acting on our instructions under a data processing agreement:

  • Cloudflare — hosting, content delivery, security, and bot protection on the contact form (Turnstile).
  • Loops — storage of contact details and delivery of email you have asked for.
  • Google Analytics 4 — site analytics, only with your consent.
  • Calendly — scheduling, if you book a call.
  • [EMAIL PROVIDER] — business correspondence.

We may also disclose personal data to professional advisers, or where required by law or to establish or defend legal claims.

Cookies

The site sets no non-essential cookies until you choose. The banner offers a genuine choice, analytics tags do not load unless you accept, and you can change your decision at any time from [link to cookie settings].
